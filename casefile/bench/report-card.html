    How this was marked

    The checkpoints come from three places: what the judge asked for in the first conversation, the stages a matter passes through under the NCLT Rules, and what courts' own systems and AI policies expect (eCourts Digital Courts 2.1, SUPACE, the Kerala High Court policy of 2025, the UK judicial guidance of 2025, Singapore's tribunal tool).

    A checkpoint counts as Working only if it exists in the live app and passed a test. Partly counts for half. The grade for an area is the share of its checkpoints met: A is 80 per cent or more, B 65, C 45, D 25, and E below that.

    Every test so far used a scripted stand-in for Claude's reading, because the app was tested outside Claude. So the marks for anything that depends on reading are provisional until real orders go through it.
